# Compact profile popover

## Outcome

Keep the operator profile surface compact on tablet and desktop-like viewports. The profile may use a bottom sheet only on phone-sized screens.

## Acceptance

- At an 800px viewport, the profile is no wider than 352px and occupies less than half the viewport.
- Existing phone-sized sheet behavior remains available at 520px and below.
- Other sidebar overlays retain their existing responsive behavior.
