# Plan 18-04 — Viewport-safe sidebar overlays

## Goal

Eliminate recurring sidebar clipping by giving every sidebar-originated floating surface one tested viewport-level rendering and collision contract.

## Tasks

1. Add a browser-safe Svelte action backed by a pure placement function. It portals the active surface to `document.body`, uses fixed coordinates, preserves an 8px viewport gutter, flips above/below its trigger based on available space, bounds height, repositions on resize and capture-phase scroll, and restores/removes cleanly.
2. Migrate profile, presence, notification feed, notification settings, and room-actions panels to the shared primitive without changing their semantic roles, close behavior, focus restoration, or compact bottom-sheet presentation.
3. Add deterministic placement unit tests and Playwright assertions at desktop, tablet, phone, and short-height viewports proving every representative panel is outside the clipped rail, remains within viewport bounds, exposes internal scrolling when needed, and restores trigger focus.
