# Plan 18-05 — Channel sound and notification controls

## Goal

Give operators one persistent global sound control and explicit per-channel overrides, while simplifying the coordinator create action.

## Tasks

1. Add validated, versioned browser-local storage for global sound and per-channel sound/notification modes, including relationship-based filtering.
2. Place the global sound toggle at the right edge of the workspace command bar; add labelled channel selects to the shared room-actions overlay and mark non-default channels.
3. Apply channel sound choices to incoming tones and channel notification choices before feed or desktop delivery.
4. Replace the visible `+ Group` coordinator action with a larger right-aligned animated `+`, preserving its accessible label.
5. Add focused unit and browser regressions for persistence, precedence, filtering, indicators, placement, and accessible names.
