<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Host/guest route data → shared RoomBrowser | Different route capabilities and exact room identities cross into one presentational component. |
| Touch/history/keyboard events → modal controller | Untrusted event ordering determines dismissal, navigation, inertness, and focus ownership. |
| Drawer → child sheet/dialog replacement | Two independently interactive modal surfaces could coexist during a transition. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-07 | Elevation of Privilege | Guest `RoomBrowser` capabilities | high | mitigate | Render host lifecycle/create/settings only from explicit supplied host callbacks; browser-test their absence in guest mode and preserve route-owned business logic. |
| T-27-08 | Tampering | Exact room selection callbacks | medium | mitigate | Carry existing composite room objects/keys unchanged through typed callbacks; test exact coordinator+room navigation and never parse visible labels. |
| T-27-09 | Elevation of Privilege | Background application under modal | medium | mitigate | Enforce a single active controller surface, inert the application root, trap focus, and unmount the prior surface before replacement. |
| T-27-10 | Denial of Service | popstate/Escape/focus listener lifecycle | medium | mitigate | Use one owned transient history token, idempotent close, fallback focus, and teardown tests across repeated open/unmount cycles. |
| T-27-11 | Tampering | Destructive scrim dismissal | medium | mitigate | Keep destructive work in native dialogs, disable outside confirmation, and expose only context-specific safe/confirm actions. |
| T-27-12 | Information Disclosure | Room browser labels/history | low | accept | The drawer renders only already-authorized local presentation records and adds no transport, storage, logging, or capability data. |
</threat_model>
