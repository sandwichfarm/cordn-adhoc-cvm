<threat_model>
## ASVS posture

OWASP ASVS L1; critical/high findings block execution. All medium threats on the relay/identity trust boundary are mitigated.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Nostr relays → contact reducer | Relay query/subscription data is untrusted until signature, kind, author, generation, and ordering checks pass. |
| active identity lifecycle → async query/subscription | Old signer work must not mutate a replacement identity's state. |
| local follow intent → signed public replacement | Concurrent operations can overwrite a full-list replaceable event unless refreshed and serialized. |
| signed replacement → relay acceptance/UI | Publication attempt is not success; state/copy must wait for acceptance. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-24-11 | Spoofing | kind-3 ingress | high | mitigate | Require kind 3, exact active author, current generation, and nostr-tools verifyEvent for query/live/signer results. |
| T-24-12 | Tampering | replacement ordering | high | mitigate | Use one higher-created_at/lower-id comparator for query, subscription, refresh, echo, and commit paths. |
| T-24-13 | Tampering | stale identity race | high | mitigate | Generation-token every async boundary and close/reset subscriptions on logout/anonymous/signer change/teardown. |
| T-24-14 | Tampering | concurrent follow full-list update | high | mitigate | Serialize refresh→merge→strictly-newer sign→validate→publish→commit and test deferred concurrent operations. |
| T-24-15 | Repudiation | premature follow success | high | mitigate | Await one relay acceptance, suppress pending echo, and leave exact prior state on total failure. |
| T-24-16 | Information Disclosure | follow diagnostics | medium | mitigate | Use locked generic UI errors and keep event content/tags, relay detail, signer material, and keys out of logs/artifacts. |
</threat_model>
